Where the sentences come from

Written by people, not generated

Language exercises are built from the Tatoeba Project, an open collection of sentences written and translated by volunteers since 2006. Lessons are ordered by how common the vocabulary actually is, so the first unit teaches words you will genuinely hear. Tatoeba is licensed CC-BY 2.0 FR and is credited in full.

Before you start

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every course and every exercise is free, on all three platforms.

Do I need an account?

You can play a demo lesson without one. Saving your streak and review queue across devices needs an account.

How does review work?

A real SM-2 spaced-repetition schedule. Each exercise gets its own interval that stretches as you keep getting it right, and resets when you don't.

Is the source available?

All of it. Lexly is MIT licensed and the full repository, including the course generator, is on GitHub.
