# Attribution

Lexly's language course content is generated from open, permissively licensed data by
`scripts/build-language-course.mjs`. No third-party language-learning app's content is
used, copied, or derived from.

## Tatoeba

Example sentences and their translations come from the [Tatoeba Project](https://tatoeba.org),
a collection of sentences contributed and translated by volunteers.

Licensed **CC-BY 2.0 FR** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

Sentences are filtered and ordered by difficulty, and some are placed into generated
exercises (word banks, fill-in-the-blank, matching pairs), but the sentence text and its
translation are reproduced as contributed.

## FrequencyWords

Word frequency lists used to order lessons from common vocabulary to rare come from
[hermitdave/FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), derived from
the OpenSubtitles corpus.

Licensed **MIT**.

## Everything else

Course content outside the `languages` category — math, science, programming, skills, and
the BC curriculum material — is original to this project.
